Outlines are generated from length, span, height, fuselage width and wing sweep. Proportions are close; details such as fairings, flaps and exact fin shape are simplified. Not an engineering drawing.

Dates are first flight and first airline service. Specifications are for the variant named on each sheet and are typical published figures; a dash means the figure was left out rather than guessed. "Built" counts are approximate. Archival drawing links open searches on Wikimedia Commons and the NASA Technical Reports Server, where many public-domain three-views and NACA/NASA test reports live.
